# Video decisions (2026-11-15)

- **Remotion 4.0.543**, reusing the installed node_modules from the spare-video project via a directory junction (`video/node_modules`, gitignored). A test frame rendered in under 15 min, so no Playwright/HTML fallback was needed. Full render took ~4 min.
- **Captures are full-page Playwright screenshots + element bounding boxes**, not recordVideo. The camera pan/zoom and the highlight boxes are animated in Remotion, so motion is smooth, there is no dead time to trim, and the boxes sit exactly on the element. Explorer pages are light-themed (the explorer has no dark mode); app pages are dark.
- The explorer's "Status" text matched the header pill first, so the status highlight uses the fixed Status row position (same layout on every tx page).
- **Scene 10 (MCP · Claude as leader) skipped.** EVIDENCE.md has a Phase 7 section, but only the vault setup: the CLAUDE_LEADER key (0xa4EA…6a55) has nonce 2 (approve + deposit) and the journal has no MCP trade entries, so there are no tx hashes to show. Showing the VO's claim without on-chain proof would be false.
- **Voice:** edge-tts 7.3.1, en-US-AndrewMultilingualNeural, rate −4%. One file per scene, generated sentence by sentence and joined with 350 ms of silence; word timings from WordBoundary events, offset by sentence. The service dropped requests repeatedly; the script retries per sentence and resumes finished scenes.
- **Timeline** (scripts/timeline.cjs → src/timeline.json) is the single source for both video and audio: scene = lead + voice + 0.6 s + dwell extras (2 s music-only open; +0.6–1.4 s on capture/stamp scenes; outro +4.5 s for the 3 s logo hold and fade). Cross-fade 12 frames. Total 142.0 s (2:22).
- **Music:** original, generated in numpy (scripts/music_mix.py): Dmaj9–Bm7–Gmaj7–A(add9) pad, plucked eighth-note arpeggio, soft bass, 96 BPM. Drop `assets/music.mp3` to replace it. pyloudnorm is not installed, so levels are set by RMS: speech ≈ −20 dB RMS, music ≈ −27 dB under the voice and ≈ −21.6 dB in voice-free parts, with 0.35 s smoothed ducking and 1.5 s / 2.5 s fades. Measured result: −17.3 LUFS integrated, −1.1 dBFS peak.
- **Code scene** renders the real `trade()` from src/MandateVault.sol (one comment line shortened to fit). The highlights follow the words "market", "size", "leverage" and "order book", landing on MarketNotAllowed, NotionalCapExceeded, LeverageCapExceeded and ReduceOnly.
- **Logo** didn't exist, so it was recreated as docs/mandate-logo.svg to the spec.
- **Final = v1.** v1 passed the frame checks, and with no scene 10 there was nothing to add. `out/mandate-demo.mp4` is a copy of `out/mandate-demo-v1.mp4`.
- **Rebuild:** `python scripts/tts.py`, `node scripts/capture.cjs`, `node scripts/timeline.cjs`, `npx remotion render src/index.ts Mandate out/video.mp4 --codec=h264 --crf=20 --x264-preset=veryfast --muted`, `python scripts/music_mix.py`, then mux with ffmpeg.
